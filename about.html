ABOUT DMC PLAYER

Your game.
Your show.
One screen.

A free Windows media player that keeps films, TV and streams in view while you play. Bring your media, find a comfortable corner and settle in.

MADE FOR GAMING SESSIONS

Keep something on.
Keep playing.

Farming, driving around FiveM or putting a few hours into your favourite game: some sessions have room for a show on the side.

DMC Player gives you a small, borderless video window you can drag to a corner and stretch to fit. Hover over the picture for playback and volume controls, then move away to let them disappear.

Turn on click-through lock and your mouse clicks go to the game underneath. Your video stays in view without becoming something else to click by accident.

HOW TO USE IT

Pick a show.
Make a little room.

01

Open something

Drop in a file, paste a direct stream, choose a Live TV channel or open a service from the File menu.

02

Go mini

Use your game in borderless windowed mode. Press Ctrl + Alt + O, then drag and resize the picture.

03

Lock it and play

Press Ctrl + Alt + L to let clicks pass through. Use the same shortcut to unlock, or double-click the unlocked picture to restore the full player.

MORE WAYS TO WATCH

Files. Streams.
Live TV.

Play local video and audio, open direct media links or pick a channel in Live TV. Optional community lists and M3U imports give you more to browse, with favourites to keep the channels you like close.

YouTube and streaming-service websites have their own place inside the player. Accounts, subscriptions, location and playback requirements still apply; community links can stop working.

Build a queue only when you want one, or host a watch session and share its invitation with friends. From version 2.8, quick chat messages sit over the video and fade after about six seconds, without blocking clicks. YouTube co-watching synchronises each person's own YouTube playback from version 2.7; subscription websites are not shared or synchronised. Free test-relay sessions last up to 60 minutes.
