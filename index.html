SMALL WHEN YOU NEED IT

A player that stays
out of your way.

Drag it to a corner. Stretch it to fit. Lock it so clicks pass through to your game. Your video keeps its own volume.

01

Your media, your choice

Open local files and direct stream links. Browse official TV and streaming websites inside the player.

02

A queue you choose

Watching something doesn't add it to a list. Use Add to queue when you want to line up a few things.

03

A link for your friends

Host a watch session, copy the invite and send it yourself. Files stream from the host; each person keeps their own volume and window size.

Free test-relay sessions last up to 60 minutes. Website playback isn't shared.

THREE QUICK STEPS

Settle in.
Press play.

Use your game in borderless windowed mode so the mini player can stay over it.

  1. 1

    Install DMC Player

    Run the Windows installer. VLC and .NET are included. Setup offers Microsoft WebView2 if you need it for TV websites.

  2. 2

    Bring something to watch

    Open a file, paste a direct stream, or choose File → Live TV and select an official service.

  3. 3

    Make a little room

    Press Ctrl + Alt + O. Drag and resize the picture, then press Ctrl + Alt + L to lock or unlock clicks.
