Mac preview 0.1.0

The Mac preview uses native macOS playback and has a smaller feature set than the Windows app. Imported M3U entries, source addresses, groups and searches are held in memory for the current session. The preview does not save a playlist library, queue or automatic watch history, and does not store a library key in Keychain. Closing the app clears its in-memory lists.

Opening a local file does not upload it to the project. Importing a remote playlist and playing a stream contact the addresses you supply. An HLS master playlist may also make a limited request to one of its listed variants to identify live or on-demand playback. Media providers receive ordinary connection information. DMC Player does not automatically upload playback or crash reports.

Experimental service pages use WKWebView with its default WKWebsiteDataStore. Providers may keep cookies, cache, sign-ins and other website data locally, including after the app closes. This browser storage is separate from the session-only playlist library. Sign out through the provider's page when needed; quitting or removing the app should not be assumed to erase website data or provider-held records. The app does not extract website passwords or account cookies.

Watch together, chat, DMC Sources, AllDebrid, add-ons, torrents, saved libraries and automatic update checks are not included in this first Mac preview. The Windows-specific storage, relay and updater sections below apply to the Windows app. See the Mac preview guide for its supported features.

Windows: saved playlist libraries

From version 2.10, importing an M3U file or playlist URL saves its supported entries in a separate playlist library. Full source addresses, provider tokens, titles, groups and favourites are stored locally in playlist-library.dat and playlist-favorites.dat, compressed and protected for your current Windows user. This is separate from the optional queue and the older Live TV lists.

Importing or refreshing a remote playlist contacts that address; playing an entry contacts its media provider. Opening the saved library does not automatically refresh remote lists, download the media files or fetch logo images. Remove a source from the library to remove its saved entries. If saving fails, the previous stored library remains intact and the new import can be used for that session.

If you explicitly add an entry to your play queue, the queue's normal full-address storage applies. Watch together for an ordinary direct stream sends its full media address to guests, including any tokens in that address. Only share a source when you intend those participants to receive it.

Windows: add-ons and torrent sources

From version 2.9, configured add-on addresses and settings are stored locally in addons.dat, encrypted for your current Windows account. Addresses may contain provider keys. DMC does not run add-on JavaScript. Search and viewing history are not saved.

Using an add-on contacts the provider you select and sends the catalogue, title or episode identifier needed for that request. Providers receive normal connection information. Cinemeta supplies the optional metadata catalogue and, from Windows 2.12, the My library artwork described below.

Playing a raw torrent source through DMC's peer-to-peer engine contacts peers, trackers and the DHT peer-discovery network. They can see your IP address and torrent activity. Uploading can occur while the source is open, including while paused. Selected media is stored temporarily in TorrentCache. Stop playback, change source, reach the end or close DMC to stop that local engine and remove its owned cache. Marked inactive cache folders left by a crash are cleaned when the next torrent starts. Sources resolved through the native AllDebrid connection use the separate flow below.

Add-on video files shared through Watch together are proxied by the host. Guests receive media bytes and a room media ID, rather than the add-on's configured address or signed media URL. They may still record what they receive. This differs from ordinary direct links opened outside the add-on browser, which share the full media address.

Windows: DMC Sources and AllDebrid

From Windows version 2.11, the built-in DMC Sources provider automatically searches the Torrents CSV public index when you select a film or TV episode. These requests contain the selected title and release year or season/episode tag, together with ordinary connection information such as your IP address. They do not contain your AllDebrid key, configured add-on addresses or account details. Disable DMC Sources in Add-ons to stop its automatic index searches. DMC does not keep a search or viewing history for these sources.

The native AllDebrid connection uses a sign-in code approved on AllDebrid's page in your browser, or an API key you enter. DMC does not ask for your AllDebrid password. Its saved key is protected for your current Windows account with DPAPI in alldebrid.dat. The native connection sends that key only to AllDebrid; it is not passed to the public search index, installed add-ons or watch-session participants. AllDebrid receives account checks, selected-source resolution requests, the selected magnet/hash or hosted link and normal connection information.

Searching for sources does not add remote transfers. Pressing Play for a selected DMC source may add that selected magnet to your AllDebrid account and wait for its video files. Cancel opening stops DMC waiting; it does not delete a transfer already added to AllDebrid. In Windows 2.12, opening Films & TV with a saved account opens My library and requests its ready videos automatically. Refresh requests that list again. Searching and grouping use the loaded list in memory. These listing requests do not add or delete transfers. Playing an entry requests a media link for the selected file from AllDebrid and contacts its media host.

Disconnect removes the key saved locally by DMC. It does not revoke the key on AllDebrid, delete remote transfers or close your provider account. Manage those items through AllDebrid. Removing local files or uninstalling DMC does not erase provider-held records. AllDebrid and the public index apply their own service rules and data practices; no fixed retention period for their records is promised here. See the connection and playback steps.

Windows: My library artwork

From Windows version 2.12, My library organises ready AllDebrid videos by cleaned title, release year and media type. The ready-file listing and grouped titles stay in memory. It does not save a cloud library, Continue watching list or viewing history, and does not add these videos to your queue. Opening the main player does not index the account or download a catalogue; the listing is requested when you open Films & TV with a saved connection or press Refresh.

Posters & details is on by default. While browsing My library, DMC searches Cinemeta using the cleaned film/show title and media type, checks the release year locally, and requests details using a matched public IMDb identifier. It attaches details only to an unambiguous title/type match, with the release year checked when known. A validated public poster host receives the image request. Those services receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address. These requests do not contain the AllDebrid key, private stream addresses or full release filenames.

Titles, descriptions and artwork caches remain in memory rather than being written to a local artwork library. Work is limited to pages of 24 cards, up to 256 metadata entries, 16 MiB/64 raw poster images and 64 decoded posters at 220 × 330 pixels. There is no bulk dataset download. The only library-view setting saved in library-view.json is the artwork on/off preference; the AllDebrid key remains in its separate encrypted store.

Turn off Posters & details in My library to cancel artwork requests and clear its caches. Local title cards and exact-file playback still work. Closing the window releases its in-memory listings and artwork. The separate Mac preview does not include this library or its artwork requests. Cinemeta and image hosts operate their own services and data practices; this page does not promise deletion of provider-held connection records.

Privacy contact

DMC Player is a free media player with a Windows application and an early Mac preview. The project operator is responsible for this website and for personal information you send directly about DMC Player. For privacy requests or questions, email d.mccomish@homehops.co.uk.

This notice covers the DMC Player download website and the Windows application and Mac preview. Hosting providers, streaming services, email providers and watch-session participants also handle information when you use their services; their own practices and notices apply to that processing.

Visiting the website and downloading

The website has no DMC account system or contact form. Its own code does not set cookies, use browser storage, run visitor analytics or advertising trackers, or embed streaming-service players. FAQ searches and the gameplay demo run in your browser. Gameplay clips are served with the site without a third-party video player. The checksum copy button writes to your clipboard only when you choose it.

The homepage requests public GitHub release metadata to show aggregate Windows installer, Windows portable and Mac package download totals. GitHub receives ordinary connection details for that request. It is sent without account credentials or the page's referrer. The counter includes repeat downloads and does not identify individual downloaders, track clicks, or add cookies or browser storage. A dated last-checked total remains visible if the current metadata cannot be retrieved.

GitHub Pages serves the website and GitHub Releases supplies the downloads. Your browser sends the information needed for those requests, such as an IP address, browser information, requested address and request time. GitHub states that Pages visitors' IP addresses are logged for security. GitHub may also make aggregate repository and download statistics available to the project owner.

Read GitHub Pages' data-collection explanation and the GitHub Privacy Statement. This notice does not promise that GitHub sets no cookies or keeps no logs.

Windows: information kept on your PC

Opening a local media file does not upload it to the project. Current versions do not automatically add ordinary file opens, direct streams or channel viewing to a saved playback-history list.

  • Queue and preferences: library.json stores the queue you deliberately create, its resume positions and player preferences. Full file paths and queued stream addresses are saved; an address can contain a private access token or embedded credentials.
  • TV lists: tv-channels.json and tv-favorites.json store explicitly loaded channels, their complete stream addresses and your favourites.
  • Website sessions: the WebProfile folder holds the embedded browser's cookies, cache and other site data.
  • Downloads and diagnostics: the Updates folder holds downloaded update installers. An error may overwrite a local last-error.txt file with technical details, which can include paths or addresses. Setup may also create normal local installation logs.

Data normally lives under %LOCALAPPDATA%\DMC Player. Existing installations may keep using %LOCALAPPDATA%\Sidecar when the DMC Player folder contains neither a library nor a browser profile. Some relay trust information is always stored under %LOCALAPPDATA%\DMC Player\Relay.

DMC Player has no automatic crash-report upload or library upload to the project. If you send a log, screenshot or media address for support, the information in it becomes part of that correspondence.

Windows: streams, TV lists and service sign-ins

Playing an internet stream contacts its host. Loading an optional community list contacts IPTV-org's GitHub-hosted list; importing a playlist URL contacts the address you supply. Merely opening the Live TV picker does not fetch a community list.

Streaming-service pages run in Microsoft WebView2. Those providers receive their normal page and playback requests, may set cookies or other site storage, and may use their own advertising or analytics. Sign-ins are managed by the provider and WebView2's local profile. DMC Player's media controls do not read password fields or extract account cookies.

Microsoft runtimes and their installation or update services may make their own requests. See Microsoft's Privacy Statement, together with the privacy information of each stream or service you choose.

Windows: update checks

From version 2.6, normal startup and six-hour intervals while the player remains open request the public update.json file from this website. The app compares versions locally. The check does not send your queue, playback details, channel lists or service sign-ins; GitHub still receives normal connection information.

An installer is downloaded from GitHub only when you choose Download update. Its size and SHA-256 are checked before saving. You separately choose whether to run Setup. Successful downloads remain on your PC until removed; incomplete downloads are normally cleaned up. No update is silently installed.

Windows: watching with friends

A watch session exchanges participant display names, the selected media title and playback information such as position, timing, rate and play/pause state. For a direct stream, the complete stream URL is shared with participants and may include an access token. For a local file, the selected file's media bytes are streamed to invited guests; its local file path stays on the host.

From version 2.7, a YouTube watch session shares the canonical public video URL containing its video ID, the selected video title and playback state. Extra page-URL parameters and fragments are removed. Each participant loads the normal YouTube page independently, with their own access and any sign-in, and contacts YouTube directly. The session does not send account sessions, cookies, passwords or YouTube media bytes to other participants. YouTube and its normal service providers still handle those participants' own requests.

From version 2.8, sending watch-session chat shares the message text, sender display name and message time with the host and the connected room participants over the existing encrypted session connection. Messages are held temporarily in memory for delivery and display. DMC Player does not automatically write a chat log, keep a central chat transcript or replay earlier messages to late joiners. The local Show messages over video choice hides the overlay on that player; it does not stop the room receiving messages. Other participants may copy, record or retain what they receive. Avoid sending private information you do not want those participants to have.

Invites contain a session endpoint and temporary access information. Keep them with the people you want to join. Ending the host session closes access through that session, but cannot remove anything a guest has already received or recorded.

Automatic internet hosting currently uses the Pinggy test relay to forward encrypted session traffic. The relay and participants' network services process connection metadata, such as network addresses and connection timing. Direct-connection mode does not use that relay. See Pinggy's Privacy Policy. DMC Player does not operate a central room database or upload a room transcript to the project.

Relay trust information can remain locally after a session. Temporary relay identity files are normally removed when the relay closes; an abnormal exit may leave temporary files behind. A free relay's session limit is an availability limit, not a promise about its log-retention period.

If you contact the project

Emailing d.mccomish@homehops.co.uk supplies your email address, message and anything you attach or choose to include. This information is used to respond, investigate the issue and manage relevant follow-up. Your email provider and the receiving email service also process that correspondence.

For personal information the project handles for support and project security, the basis is legitimate interests in responding to requests, maintaining the application and protecting users. Information may also be used where needed to comply with a legal obligation. Contact is voluntary; an answer may not be possible without enough information to understand the request.

Correspondence is kept only while reasonably needed for the request, relevant follow-up, disputes or legal duties. This notice does not impose an invented fixed retention period on independent service providers.

Retention, deletion and your choices

On Windows, local preferences, manual queues, imported channels, favourites, browser data, downloaded installers and diagnostics generally remain until you change or remove them. Removing or clearing a queue updates the saved queue; signing out on a service page handles that provider's session. Uninstalling DMC Player should not be assumed to erase its app-data folders.

For a Windows local reset, close the player first, then remove the relevant files or WebProfile folder from the DMC Player or legacy Sidecar data folder. Removing those files loses the saved settings or sign-ins they contain. The separate DMC Player relay folder, installation logs and downloaded packages may need separate removal. Provider-held account records are not deleted by removing local files.

You control which streams, providers, playlists and watch sessions you use. Avoid sharing URLs, logs or screenshots containing information you do not intend to disclose. There is no DMC marketing list, sale of your library or advertising profile created by DMC Player's own code, and no automated decision-making with legal or similarly significant effects.

Your rights and third-party processing

Where data-protection law applies, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal information, and to object to processing based on legitimate interests. Rights depend on the processing and are not unlimited. Email d.mccomish@homehops.co.uk about information the project holds; information kept only on your PC is managed through your device, and requests about a provider's own records should go to that provider.

GitHub, Microsoft, Pinggy, streaming providers and email services may process information in countries outside the UK or your country. Their privacy notices describe their locations, safeguards and retention practices. DMC Player does not promise UK-only storage for those independent services.

You may complain to your local data-protection authority. In the UK, this is the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). You can contact the project first, but doing so does not remove that right.

Changes to this notice

This notice describes the current website and application. It may be updated when features, providers or data practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision. The Terms & conditions explain the conditions for using DMC Player.
