Watch sessions and relay limits

Hosting makes the selected media available to the people you invite. Keep the invitation private and obtain any permission needed to share the content. Participants receive playback information and, for direct streams, the full media address. Do not share an address containing private credentials or tokens unless you intend the participants to receive it.

Keep the host player open while watching. Stopping the session expires that session's access, but does not recall content already received. YouTube co-watching synchronizes the public video page and playback state from version 2.7; each person loads YouTube independently with their own access. YouTube media bytes and account sessions are not relayed. Subscription websites are not relayed or synchronized. Ads, loading, autoplay and regional or account restrictions may delay or prevent playback, and a participant may need to press Play on YouTube.

Watch-session chat is available from version 2.8. Sending a message shares its text, display name and message time with the host and connected participants. DMC Player does not automatically save a transcript or replay earlier chat to late joiners, but another participant may copy or record messages. Hiding messages locally does not recall anything already received.

The automatic public relay is a free testing service, currently limited to sessions of up to 60 minutes. Availability, speed and capacity are not guaranteed. Relay operators can change or withdraw their service. Do not rely on the test relay for a service that needs guaranteed availability.
